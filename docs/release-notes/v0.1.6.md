**Health replaces Water.** The Water page, its hourly reminder, the "+250 ml" and "+500 ml" buttons and the "Water reminders" switch in the tray menu are gone. In their place is a Health page for your steps: today against your goal on a green ring, distance and active energy, and your week as bars.

The steps come from your phone: Oyenza reads them from Apple Health or Health Connect. Signing in with your Oyenza account is coming soon. Until then, the Health page explains how it will work. Once you're signed in, the island will also tell you when your steps reach the daily goal.

**Your drinks are kept.** Everything you logged on the Water page stays in your data file, untouched. If Oyen Poni last showed Water, it now opens Health.

**Install:** download `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Poni`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
