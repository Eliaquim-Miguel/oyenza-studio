**Add-ons: features you download only if you use them.** The new **+** at the end of the tabs lists them. Switching one on downloads its files from this project's releases, each checked against the fingerprint built into the app. Switching it off deletes them. While every add-on is off, nothing is downloaded.

**Prayer times, the first add-on.** Today's times for your kab/kota as Kemenag RI publishes them, the next prayer and how long until it, and the qibla. Windows' location picks the nearest of Kemenag's 517 places and checks again every few hours, so a trip moves the place. The point is used once and stays on your PC. You can also type your kab/kota. Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah use Umm al-Qura. The island counts down the 10 minutes before each prayer and says when it's time, with a short tone, the adzan recorded at Masjidil Haram, or nothing. Music pauses for the adzan, and Stop ends it. Checked against Kemenag's 2026 schedule: 98.2% of 227,367 times match to the minute, and none is more than a minute off. Switching it on downloads 1.9 MB.

**Your Oyenza, live, by day.** The Oyenza tab now lists every transaction of the last 7 days, money in and money out, not only money that came in by itself. Pick a day on the strip (Sun to Thu, Yesterday or Today) to see it newest first, with that day's money in and out. Something logged while you look glows for a minute. **Tomorrow** and **Later** list the subscriptions and card bills due in the next 30 days. While the tab is open it checks Oyenza every 15 seconds; closed, it doesn't.

**Money out, too.** When Oyenza logs spending by itself, like a card charge read from your bank's email, the island says "Money out · BCA", next to the money-in notice. Sums stay hidden until you click Show, there and in the tab.

What Oyen Poni's key reads changes with this: your last 7 days of transactions (titles, sums, categories, wallets and times), never balances, account numbers, notes or receipts. oyenza.app's approve screen and privacy page say so.

**The System page, redone.** It now reads Windows' own kernel counters, the way system monitors on the Mac read macOS, and lays them out in rings and charts:

- **CPU:** the load split between Windows and apps, the temperature (or the processor's power) when your PC reports it, the speed, one bar per core with performance and efficiency cores apart, and the eight processes using the most.
- **GPU (new):** the busiest engine, the 3D, copy and video engines, video memory, and the temperature, fan and clock when the driver reports them.
- **Memory:** pressure on a gauge, and what fills memory: apps, wired, compressed, cached and free.
- **Drives, network and battery:** read and write speeds with the processes reading and writing the most, download and upload side by side with the interface, address and link speed, and the battery's health against when it was new.

A strip across the top shows every part's live value over its last minute; click one to open it. The charts are full the moment the page opens: the last minute keeps going while it's closed, with one light reading a second (about a third of a millisecond of processor time). Right-click › **Keep System page history** turns that off. The Task Manager button is gone. No driver, no admin rights, and nothing leaves your PC.

**Drag the time bar to jump anywhere in a song.** Hold the time bar on the Music page and slide: the knob and both times follow your pointer, and the song jumps there when you let go, even off the bar. A click still jumps straight there.

**Oyen Poni for Mac, a test build.** Each release now carries `Oyen-Poni-Mac.dmg` beside the Windows installer, for macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel: the island, music with synced lyrics from Music and Spotify, the focus timer, the Shelf with Phone drop over the same Wi-Fi, tasks and clipboard history. Glass, paired phones, Oyenza sign-in and add-ons aren't on the Mac yet, and its phone panel now says "this Mac" instead of "this PC".

**Install:** download `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyen-Poni-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyen Poni into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Poni`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
