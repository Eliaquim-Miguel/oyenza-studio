**Pill, a fourth theme.** Right-click the island, then **Theme › Pill**, or pick it under Theme in Settings. The island floats a little below the top of the screen as a black capsule, and everything it shows has a layout of its own:

- At rest: the time on the left and, on a laptop, the battery on the right, with a bolt while it's plugged in. A desktop shows the time alone.
- **Status bar**, a new idle look in Pill (right-click › **Idle look › Status bar**): the volume, the date and time, and the battery.
- Music: the line being sung, or the title and artist between lines, with bars in the album's colour and a glow of it along the bottom.
- The focus timer, the minutes before a prayer, the readout over games, the volume, charging and Bluetooth pop-ups and notices are capsules too.
- Open, it's a card with every corner rounded. The Music page puts the art, the player's name, the song, the time left and big controls side by side, with the lyrics beside them.

Pill moves its own way: it stretches into a wider capsule before it drops open and flattens back when it closes. A pointer thrown at the top edge still opens it, and clicks beside it reach the window under it. A soft shadow and a fine rim keep it apart from the wallpaper.

**On a Mac** Pill works too. On a MacBook with a notch, the resting island and the timer stay beside the camera, and the line being sung, pop-ups, notices and the open island float just below the notch.

Classic stays the default. Oyen and Glass are unchanged.

**Install:** download `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyen-Poni-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyen Poni into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Poni`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
