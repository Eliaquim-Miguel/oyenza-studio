**Show over games no longer slows games down.** In 0.1.18 the readout stayed over the game, and any window over a borderless game makes Windows combine the two before they reach the screen, which costs about a frame of delay. Now the island hides over the game, so its frames go straight to the screen again, and the readout shows for a few seconds: when a game comes to the front, when its frame rate drops sharply, and whenever you press **Alt+Shift+F** (press it again to hide it sooner). Alt+Shift+F belongs to Oyen Poni only while a game is in front. Glass no longer copies the screen while a full-screen app is in front; it draws smoked glass there.

**A smaller download.** The Windows installer is about 2.4 MB, down from 6.3 MB. The Japanese reading dictionary moved out of the app into an add-on, and the parts that run only for moments, such as phone pairing and QR codes, are now built for size. The Mac download is unchanged.

**Japanese readings, only if you need them.** Kanji are read with Windows' own reading analyzer. On a PC without Windows' Japanese data, kana lines still read right away, and the Music page offers Japanese readings under the lyrics: a 3.8 MB add-on that reads every kanji line. One click downloads it. It's in the Add-ons tab too, and switching it off there deletes the file. PCs where Windows reads Japanese never see the offer. On a Mac nothing changes: the dictionary stays built in.

**Apple Music's progress bar and back button work.** Apple Music for Windows tells Windows' media controls that seeking and going back are off, so the island's bar did nothing and Back was greyed out. With Apple Music, the island now moves Apple Music's own progress slider and presses its own back button: drag the bar, click a lyric line to jump to it, or press Back.

**Lyrics and lists glide.** On Windows, moving text used to land on whole pixels, so a line easing into place stood still, then jumped a pixel. Lyrics and the Tasks, Clipboard and Oyenza lists now glide between pixels and look exactly as before once they stop. On Windows and the Mac, the line being sung grows smoothly instead of in small steps, and each lyric scroll starts gently.

**The System page rests while you're away.** Its last minute of history (a light reading once a second) stops while your PC is locked, the screen is off or battery saver is on, and starts a fresh minute when you're back.

**Install:** download `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyen-Poni-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyen Poni into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Poni`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
